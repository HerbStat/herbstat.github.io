HerbStat Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

HerbStat does not collect any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server to send anything to.

The short version. The entire research database is stored inside the app on your device. Everything you search, tap or change stays on your phone. We have no way of knowing that you use HerbStat, let alone what you look up.

Information we collect

None. HerbStat has no sign-in, no profile, no contact form and no telemetry. We do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, location, health information, usage statistics, or crash reports.

Information stored on your device

So the app can behave sensibly between launches, it stores a small amount of data locally, in the app's own storage:

  • The research database that ships with the app.
  • The items you save with the star, your weighting settings and appearance choice, and whether you have seen the first-run notice.

This never leaves your phone. We cannot read it. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Subscriptions

HerbStat is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles the purchase, the payment and any renewal or cancellation. HerbStat never receives your name, email address, payment details, or Apple Account or Google Account information. To know whether to open, the app asks the store on your device (Apple's StoreKit, or Google Play Billing) whether a HerbStat subscription is active. Each store's handling of purchases is covered by that company's own privacy policy.

Network connections

HerbStat works offline. Apart from the App Store or Google Play checking your subscription, it makes no network requests of its own.

There is one exception, and it is under your control: when you tap a study's PubMed or DOI link, your phone opens that address in your browser. At that point you have left HerbStat, and the site you have opened — PubMed, run by the US National Library of Medicine, or the publisher behind a DOI — will see an ordinary web request from your browser in the normal way. Their handling of that request is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children

HerbStat is a reference work for adults. It is not directed at children, and because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose personal information, because we do not have any. There are no analytics services, trackers or advertising networks in the app.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase. Data held on your own device is removed by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of HerbStat ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly here rather than buried.

Not medical advice

HerbStat reports what has been published in the research literature. It does not tell you what to take, how much, or whether anything is safe or appropriate for you. Herbs and supplements interact with medicines and with one another. Speak to a doctor or pharmacist before acting on anything you read in the app.

Contact
