About

The B vitamin B9, in two broad kinds: folate is the umbrella term for natural forms found in leafy greens, legumes and liver, mostly as polyglutamates, while folic acid is the synthetic, fully oxidised form used in supplements and in mandatory flour fortification in many countries. Folic acid is more stable and better absorbed than food folate but must be converted in the body to active forms such as 5-methyltetrahydrofolate, which is also sold directly; studies of dietary folate and folic acid are therefore not interchangeable.
