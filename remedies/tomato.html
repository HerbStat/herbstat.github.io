About

The fruit of Solanum lycopersicum (formerly Lycopersicon esculentum), a member of the nightshade family domesticated in the Americas and a staple of cuisines worldwide. It is the principal dietary source of the red carotenoid lycopene. Lycopene is absorbed far better from cooked and processed tomato products eaten with fat, such as sauces and paste, than from raw tomatoes, so studies of tomato intake and tomato products are not straightforwardly comparable.
