About

The aromatic oleo-gum resin of Boswellia trees of the torchwood family. Boswellia carterii, now generally treated as Boswellia sacra, grows in Somalia, Oman and Yemen and supplies the frankincense traded since antiquity for incense; the related Indian species, Boswellia serrata, is used in Ayurveda and supplies most supplement extracts. Research concentrates on the boswellic acids, especially AKBA, which are non-volatile, so distilled frankincense essential oil contains little or none of them. HerbStat files research on both B. carterii (sacra) and B. serrata here, as well as on related frankincense species, because most supplements and studies use B. serrata; check each paper for the species and extract tested.
