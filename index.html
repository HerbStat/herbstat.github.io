HerbStat

An index of published research on herbs, foods and supplements, for iPhone and iPad

HerbStat counts what has been published. For 979 remedies and 324 conditions it shows how many papers exist, what they found, and what kind of studies they were — in people, in animals, or only in the lab — with a link to every paper on PubMed.

This is a research index, not medical advice. HerbStat does not tell you what to take, how much, or whether anything is safe or right for you. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before acting on anything you read in it.

Support

Questions, corrections or a paper you think is missing: write to herbstat@tuta.com. Corrections to the data are especially welcome; each one is read.

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection? No. The whole database is inside the app. Only the PubMed and DOI links open the web.

What does the score mean? It is a weighted count of papers, not a verdict. A high score means a lot has been published, not that something works. You can change every weight in Settings, and turn on “Human studies only”.

How often is it updated? The research is refreshed from PubMed and shipped as an app update. Your saved items are kept across updates.

How does the subscription work? HerbStat is $9.99 a month or $99 a year (or the equivalent in your currency), billed by Apple and renewed automatically until you cancel. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions; cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. On a new device, open HerbStat and tap Restore Purchases.

Where are my saved items stored? On your device only. HerbStat has no account and collects nothing — see the privacy policy.
